Craft Your Love Story with a Custom Engagement Ring

Your love is one of a kind - your ring should be too. At Charles & Colvard®, we believe that every love story deserves a symbol as unique as the bond it represents. That's why we invite you to build your own engagement ring, a personalized expression of your commitment and style.

Build Your Own Ring

Why Build Your Own Ring?

A REFLECTION
OF YOUR LOVE

No two love stories are the same. Designing your own engagement ring exures that it embodies the essence of your journey together. Choose a setting that speaks to your heart and pair it with a stunning lab-grown gemstone that aligns with your values and aesthetics.

EXPERTLY CRAFTED,
ETHICALLY SOURCED

At Charles & Colvard®, we're pioneers in ethical fine jewelry. Our premium Forever One™ moissanite and Caydia® lab-grown diamonds offer dazzling brilliance eithout compromise. You can cofidently design a ring that is as sustainable as it is breathtaking.

THE PERFECT FIT
FOR YOUR BUDGET

Customizing your engagement ring means you control every aspect of the design, ensuring that you get the perfect ring at the right price. Choose the setting, the gemstone, and the details that matter most to you -- all while staying within your budget.

A MEANINGFUL
EXPERIENCE

Building your own engagement ring transforms the shopping process into a heartfelt journey. Every decision is infused with significance, from selecting a setting to choosing the ideal gem. The result? A ring with a story as beautiful as the love it represents.

GET STARTED NOW

How It Works:

1. Start with a Setting or a Gemstone

Whether you envision a timeless solitaire, a vintage-inspired halo, or a sleek modern design, begin by choosing the perfect foundation - or let the gemstone guide your selection.

View Settings

2. Select Your Center Stone

Choose from our brilliant Forever One™ moissanite or responsibly-grown diamonds in various shapes, sizes and cuts.

View Stones

3. Make It Yours

Personalize your ring with your metal of choice, including 14K white gold, 14K yellow gold, or 14K rose gold. Add finishing touches that make it uniquely yours.

View Settings

4. Cherish It Forever

Once your custom design is complete, our expert artisans will bring your vision to life with precision and care, creating a masterpiece to cherish for a lifetime.
