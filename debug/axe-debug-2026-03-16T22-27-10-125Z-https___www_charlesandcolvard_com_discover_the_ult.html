The Ultimate Lab-Grown Engagament Ring Guide

At Charles & Colvard®, we believe your love story deserves a ring that reflects your commitment and values. Our Caydia® lab-grown diamonds and Forever One™ moissanite offer stunning brilliance, sustainable sourcing, and exceptional quality—without the environmental or ethical concerns of mined diamonds. This guide will help you find the perfect engagement ring while highlighting all the benefits of lab-grown jewelry.

Shop Engagement Rings

Why Choose Lab-Grown Diamonds & Moissanite?

  • ETHICALLY SOURCED:
    No mining means no environmental destruction or human rights concerns.

  • EXCEPTIONAL VALUE:
    Enjoy a larger, more brilliant gem, no matter your budget.

  • SUSTAINABLY MADE:
    Our gems are created in controlled environments with minimal environmental impact.

  • UNPARALLELED BEAUTY:
    Lab-grown diamonds are chemically, optically, and physically the same as mined diamonds; the only difference is their origin. With a high refractive index of 2.65, moissanite is the world’s most brilliant gem™.

Choosing the Perfect Shape

Each gem shape offers unique beauty and style. Whether you prefer the traditional elegance of a round brilliant cut or you love the uniqueness of a fancy cut, such as a radiant or pear, Charles & Colvard® has something for everyone.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT:

Timeless, classic, and the most popular gem shape and cut.

PERFECT FOR:

Those who love traditional elegance and the most brilliant sparkle.

The oval shape elongates the finger and maximizes carat weight appearance.

Cushion

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT:

Soft, rounded edges with a vintage feel and incredible brilliance.

PERFECT FOR:

Those seeking brilliant sparkle and light reflection.

SHOP CUSHION

Emerald

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT:

Sophisticated, clean lines show off a “hall of mirrors” effect.

PERFECT FOR:

Minimalists and those who appreciate understated luxury.

SHOP EMERALD

Princess

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT:

Sharp angles and brilliant sparkle in a modern square shape.

PERFECT FOR:

Those who want a contemporary yet dazzling look.

Pear

Marquise

Radiant
