  • FAQs

    • How do I find the right ring size?

      Measure an existing ring or use our free printable sizing guide. For accuracy, visit a jeweler or use a ring sizer tool. We offer complimentary resizing with every purchase to ensure a perfect fit.

    • Which engagement rings are popular?

      Solitaire, halo, and vintage-inspired designs top the trends. Moissanite and lab-grown diamond rings are increasingly popular for their brilliance and affordability. Explore our bestsellers for timeless yet modern styles.

    • Are engagement ring and wedding ring the same?

      No. Engagement rings symbolize the proposal and are typically more elaborate. Wedding bands are exchanged during the ceremony and are simpler. Many wear both together after marriage. We offer matching sets for a cohesive look.

    • Does engagement ring go on right hand?

      Traditionally, engagement rings are worn on the left ring finger in Western cultures. Some countries (e.g., Germany, Russia) use the right hand. Ultimately, it’s personal preference—we’ll customize any ring for your chosen hand.

    • What is the most popular design for an engagement ring?

      The classic solitaire remains the most sought-after style, prized for its timeless elegance. Halo settings closely follow, offering extra sparkle. Our collection blends these favorites with unique, ethically crafted designs.
